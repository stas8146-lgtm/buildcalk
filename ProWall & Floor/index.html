Frequently Asked Questions

How much extra tile should I buy for waste?

For a standard straight layout, add 5%. For large format tiles or diagonal layouts, add 7-10%. Herringbone patterns usually require up to 10-15% extra for waste.

How much tile adhesive do I need per m²?

Standard consumption is about 1.3 kg per m² for every 1mm of adhesive thickness. With a 10mm notched trowel, expect about 4-5mm of solid glue, meaning ~5-6.5 kg per m².

How are SVP levelling clips calculated?

The amount of clips depends on the tile dimensions. Smaller tiles require more clips per square meter. Wedges are reusable, so you only need enough for your daily laying rate.
