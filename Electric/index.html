❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Demand Factor in electrical calculations?

The demand factor takes into account that not all electrical outlets and devices will be used simultaneously at their maximum capacity. Our calculator adjusts the total power automatically based on the number of sockets.

How is the cable cross-section recommended?

The cross-section is calculated based on the effective amperage, conductor material (Copper or Aluminum), and the voltage type (230V or 400V). The tool selects the safest standard cross-section (e.g., 1.5mm², 2.5mm²).
