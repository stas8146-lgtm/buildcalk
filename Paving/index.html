❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much waste margin should I add for paving?
Usually, a 5% margin is standard for straight patterns. For diagonal patterns or complex curves, consider 8-10% to account for cutting.
What is the standard depth for gravel subbase?
For pedestrian paths, 10-15 cm is sufficient. For driveways and vehicular traffic, 20-30 cm of crushed stone is recommended.
