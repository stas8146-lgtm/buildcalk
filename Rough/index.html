Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is the unified master estimate calculated?
The master estimate aggregates all structural elements—foundation, masonry, roof systems, labor, and logistics—into a single transparent budget summary.
What is included in the rough construction price?
It covers core shell elements including concrete works, rebar, structural blocks, mortar, timber framing, and contractor labor fees.
