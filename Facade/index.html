❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much insulation waste should I account for?

Typically, a 5% waste margin is recommended for standard walls, and up to 10% for complex facades with many windows.

EPS vs Mineral Wool: Which is better?

EPS is cheaper and easier to install, while mineral wool offers superior fire resistance and acoustic insulation.

How many dowels are needed per square meter?

Standard application requires 5 to 6 dowels per m². For higher buildings or edge zones, this number increases to 8-12.

What is an APU strip and why do I need it?

APU strips connect the facade plaster to the window frame elastically, preventing cracks and water ingress.
