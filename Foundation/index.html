❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Strip vs. Slab Foundation?

Strip foundations support load-bearing walls and are cost-effective. Slab foundations provide a solid flat base for the entire structure, ideal for weak soils.

How much rebar is needed per m³ of concrete?

On average, standard residential foundations require about 80 kg of rebar per cubic meter of concrete, though precise structural calculations may vary.
