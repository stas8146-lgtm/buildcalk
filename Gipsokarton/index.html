Frequently Asked Questions

How many drywall sheets do I need per m²?

Typically, one sheet covers 3.0 m² (1.2x2.5m). With a standard 10% waste factor, you divide your surface area by sheet size and multiply by layers.

What profile spacing should I choose?

600 mm spacing is standard for regular wall claddings and ceilings. Choose 400 mm for heavy-duty walls or tiled surfaces requiring higher rigidity.

Should I use 1 or 2 layers of drywall?

1 layer is standard for residential partition walls and ceilings. 2 layers provide superior soundproofing, fire resistance, and impact strength.
