Frequently Asked Questions

How many water points or radiators?

Count each terminal fixture (sink, toilet, shower) or individual heating panel radiator required for your layout.

What pipe material is best?

PEX-a and Al-PEX are most flexible for modern residential setups, while PPR is welded and copper offers premium durability.

How is the unified estimate calculated?

The calculator combines total material needs, installation labor, contractor markup, and logistics into a unified project estimate.
