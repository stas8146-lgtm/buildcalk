About Professional Fencing Calculation

Accurate estimation of fencing materials prevents unnecessary expenses and project delays. Our online calculator allows contractors and homeowners to precisely calculate the number of support posts, panel filling area, concrete volume for post holes and strip foundations, as well as total installation labor costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the number of posts calculated?
The number of posts is determined by dividing the total perimeter length by the chosen post step distance, rounding up, and adding one final post.
Does the calculation include strip foundation concrete?
Yes, the tool computes both individual post hole concrete and continuous strip foundation volume based on your width and depth inputs.
