❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the soil swelling coefficient?

When soil is excavated, it loosens and takes up more space. A coefficient of 1.25 means the volume increases by 25%. This is crucial for calculating dump truck trips.

How to calculate dump truck trips?

Divide the loose soil volume by the truck capacity. Our calculator does this automatically and rounds up to the nearest full trip.
